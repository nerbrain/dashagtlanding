THE WHOLE STACK

Frontend, backend and mobile. One platform.

Most no-code tools stop at the screen. dashAGT gives you the database, the business logic, the file storage and the mobile app too, so one person can build the internal tools that used to need a development team.

UI

Interface

Drag-and-drop pages with layouts for desktop, tablet and phone.

LOGIC

Business logic

Queries, validations, workflows, approval chains and scheduled jobs.

DATA

Database and files

Managed tables, or your own Postgres, MySQL, SQL Server and REST APIs. Uploads and generated PDFs included.

SHIP

Web and Android

Publish once. It's live on the web and in the dashAGT Android app.

HOW IT WORKS

From spreadsheet to working app in an afternoon.

01

Model your data

Create tables and relationships, import Excel, or connect a database you already run.

02

Design the screens

Drag in tables, forms, charts and uploads, and bind them to your data.

03

Add the logic

Wire up actions, approval chains, workflows and document templates.

04

Publish

Your staff open it on the web and on Android straight away. Update anytime.

BUILDING BLOCKS

Pieces that fit together into any app.

Component library

Tables, forms, inputs, charts, uploads, modals and more, each configurable without code.

Action library

Run queries, save records, navigate, notify people and call webhooks on any event.

Approval chains

Multi-step approvals with conditions, quorum, delegation and escalation.

Document generation

Your templates filled from your data and turned into numbered, verifiable PDFs.

Files, photos and video

Uploads attached to the right record, resumable on slow connections.

Workflows and notifications

When something happens, assign tasks and notify by email, SMS or WhatsApp.

NATIVE MOBILE, INCLUDED

Every internal app, on every phone your team carries.

Your staff install dashAGT from the Play Store once, sign in to your organization, and see exactly the apps you’ve given them. Publish a change and it reaches every phone, with no app store release.

  • —Camera, GPS and signatures built in
  • —Push notifications and approvals on the go
  • —Fingerprint unlock
